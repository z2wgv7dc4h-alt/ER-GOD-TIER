# PS5 capture tips — getting a clean screenshot read

The app reads a **phone photo of your TV**. That is the supported capture path: a
PlayStation save cannot be dropped here and the PS App / Share-button route is not
part of this workflow. A good photo reads every number; a rushed one still reads
most of it, because matching is fuzzy and label-anchored rather than tied to fixed
pixel positions.

## The four things that matter

1. **Shoot straight-on.** Stand square to the TV, not off to one side. Perspective
   skew is the biggest cause of a missed line, though the reader corrects mild skew.
   If the menu looks like a trapezoid in the photo, walk around in front of it.
2. **Fill the frame.** Get the whole menu inside the photo with little else around
   it. Small text is the enemy: the closer you are, the larger every numeral is to
   the OCR engine. Do **not** zoom with the camera — move closer instead.
3. **Kill the glare.** Turn off or move lamps behind you, close bright curtains, and
   tilt the phone slightly until the reflection slides off the screen. A washed-out
   patch of TV hides whatever is underneath it.
4. **Tap to focus.** Tap the middle of the menu on the phone screen and wait for the
   focus to settle before you shoot. A soft, blurry photo loses the thin strokes in
   numbers like 4 and 9.

## Which screens to capture

| Screen | Where it is | What it reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Status** | Character menu → Status | Name, Level, Runes Held/Needed, all eight stats (base stats are corrected for talisman bonuses) |
| **Equipment** | Character menu → Equipment | The selected slot name, weapon affinity and upgrade, and the grid icons/counts |
| **Inventory** | Character menu → Inventory | Key items, Great Runes, Bell Bearings, Cookbooks |
| **Warp list / map** | Map menu | Sites of Grace you have lit |
| **Remembrance / banner** | After a boss | Which fight is down |

## Reading the Status screen

The Status screen shows your stats **including** bonuses from talismans, helmets and
activated Great Runes, but the Level row is always the base-stat sum minus 79. The
app spots the difference and shows you the base spread plus the gear it removed —
for example, *“showing base stats — your Status screen includes +5 Vig/End/Str/Dex
from Radagon’s Soreseal.”* Nothing is stored as a stat it did not read.

## If a shot will not read

- Take it again straight-on and closer.
- Photograph the same screen twice; the reader merges both attempts.
- You can always type or paste the names you can see — the Type/paste box on each
  step matches them the same way.
