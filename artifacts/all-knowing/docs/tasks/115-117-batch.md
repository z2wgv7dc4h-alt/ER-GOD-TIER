## 115 — Peek cards + term tooltips (owns `src/library/EntityLink.tsx`, new `src/peek/*`, `src/lib/glossary.ts`, `src/knowledge/mechanics.ts`)
1. **Peek card** for every `EntityLink`: desktop hover (300ms delay) / keyboard focus, phone long-press (450ms)
   shows a compact card: icon, name, kind, status for this character (owned/defeated/available/locked),
   2–4 key facts by kind (weapon: attack at my stats, scaling letters, requirements ✓/✗, weight; armor:
   negations/poise/weight; talisman: effect; boss: weak to / resists, HP, recommended level; NPC: where now +
   quest step; grace: region, discovered?), and "Open" / "Show on map". A normal tap still opens the full page.
   One shared portal, repositions to stay on-screen, closes on scroll/escape/outside tap.
2. **Term tooltips**: a `<Term id>` component for jargon, auto-applied by `glossary.autolink` to mechanic terms:
   Attack/AR, scaling letters (S/A/B/C/D/E with the numeric ranges), soft cap, poise, equip load classes,
   negation, status buildup, FP, stamina, Rune Arc, Scadutree blessing, affinity. Short 1–2 sentence
   definitions from `mechanics.ts`. Apply `<Term>` to the labels in the entity Stats tab, Build planner, stat
   planner, Gear sheet and Library table headers.
3. Tests: peek content per kind, glossary term detection.

## 116 — PvP & OP content depth (owns `src/knowledge/pvp.ts`, `src/knowledge/builds.ts`, new `src/knowledge/pvpTech.ts`, the Kit PvP/OP render components)
Research allowed (community sources: Fextralife, wiki, reddit meta threads; personal project, cite `source`).
1. **PvP builds**: at least 4 per bracket for RL30-50, RL60-90, RL125, RL150 (+ DLC-era variants) — each with
   full loadout (weapons + affinity + ash of war, armor, 4 talismans, spells, consumables/buff order), exact
   stat spread that sums to the bracket level (validated by a test: sum − 79 = level), playstyle, combos,
   what it beats / loses to, and patch stamp. Mark any that depend on a nerfed patch.
2. **Matchups**: counters for the 25 most common PvP threats (e.g. Rivers of Blood/Blasphemous Blade legacy,
   bleed katanas, Comet Azur, Freezing Mist spam, Bloodhound Step, colossal jump attacks, Carian Slicer, pot
   builds, Moonveil, Hand of Malenia, Mimic ganks, Black Flame, gank squads) — tell, counter, gear swap.
3. **PvP tech** (`pvpTech.ts`): backstab/riposte setups, parry windows by weapon class, roll-catching,
   delayed attacks, chain backstabs, invasion tactics (items: Taunter's Tongue, Festering Bloody Finger, Blue
   Cipher Ring), ganks, duel etiquette, dodge timing, 2H-ing, crouch pokes; each with a short how-to.
4. **OP PvE**: each OP kit gets a one-tap "level plan" (stat spread at levels 40/60/100/150) and the route to
   get its pieces (reuse `buildHunt`).
5. Kit UI renders the new fields: brackets as filter chips; build cards collapsed to name + bracket + 1-line
   pitch, expand for full loadout; "Use this build" → change-build plan.
6. Tests: stat sums, every item id resolves in the entity graph, no duplicate ids.
